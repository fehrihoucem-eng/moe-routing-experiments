Which experts fire, and when

Expert routing in Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, measured over corpus_v1 — 100 prompts, five categories, greedy decoding. Everything below is the train split; the test split is held out.

1 · The shape

Every token picks 8 experts in each of 40 layers

The model has 40 layers. Each layer holds its own 256 experts and, for every token, its router selects 8 of them and gives each a gate — a weight, and the eight sum to 1. One token therefore lights up 320 experts out of 10,240.

2 · The distinction that matters

An expert is a (layer, slot) pair — not a slot number

Each layer numbers its own experts 0–255. Slot 169 in layer 0 and slot 169 in layer 16 are different weight matrices that never share a parameter. colibri counts them the way you should: 40 × 256 = 10,240 distinct experts.

4 · What was measured

100 prompts, split 80/20 by prompt

Four test prompts per category are held out. The split is by prompt, never by token: tokens inside one answer share a prefix and a topic, so splitting those across train and test would leak.

Decode tokens per category

Answer length varies, so equal prompt counts do not give equal token counts. Structured extraction answers are simply shorter.
